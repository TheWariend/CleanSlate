# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want CleanSlate to be a project people are glad to spend their time on. Everyone is welcome to participate regardless of experience level, background, identity, or how they found their way here.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith
- Give and receive feedback about the work, not the person
- Accept that maintainers make the final call on scope and direction
- Respect people's time: search before asking, keep discussions focused, and write your own words

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests
- Spam, including bulk low-quality or automated contributions

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any official channel — and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior by opening a confidential report through GitHub's [private vulnerability and abuse reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam), or by contacting a maintainer directly.

All reports are reviewed and investigated. Reporters' identities are kept confidential.

Maintainers may take any action they judge appropriate, including warnings, removal of contributions, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project. Maintainers who do not follow this code in good faith may face the same consequences.
